Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 Aug 2026

1. About Music Fever

Music Fever is an academic software project developed as part of a Bachelor's Degree Final Project in Computer Engineering.
The service allows users to search for music, create and manage playlists, participate in collaborative Rooms, submit music requests, view personal statistics and, where applicable, connect an external music account for playback functionality.
For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the processing of personal data, you may contact:
  • Email: privacy@musicfever.com
  • Responsible entity: Universidad Rey Juan Carlos/ Arminda García

2. Information We Collect

When you create a Music Fever account, we may store:
  • Username
  • Email address
  • Password in securely protected form
  • Profile picture
  • Account creation date
Music Fever does not require users to provide their real name.

3. Music Account Connection

Music Fever may allow users to connect an external music service account.

Spotify Spotify is currently used to provide playback functionality for users who host Rooms.
Music Fever requests only the authorization required to interact with Spotify playback services. Spotify uses OAuth scopes to limit which permissions third-party applications receive.
Music Fever may temporarily retain:
  • the authorization/session information required to interact with Spotify;
  • information indicating whether the connected Spotify account supports the required playback functionality.
Music Fever does not intentionally store the user's Spotify password or complete Spotify account profile.
Some Spotify playback endpoints require Spotify Premium.
Users may disconnect their Spotify account from Music Fever.

Apple Music Music Fever currently uses Apple Music as an external source of music catalogue information.
At this stage, Music Fever does not require users to connect an Apple Music account.
A future version may allow Apple Music accounts to be linked for playback in a similar way to Spotify. Apple Music requires user authorization when an application accesses subscriber-specific music data.
If this functionality is introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

4. Rooms

Music Fever Rooms are collaborative music queues designed for people sharing the same physical space.
When a Room is created or used, Music Fever may store:
  • Room name and identifier
  • Host
  • Session date
  • Participants
  • Songs added by participants
  • User responsible for each song addition
  • Time at which songs were added
  • Songs played and not played
  • Room duration
  • Room statistics and aggregated activity data
This information is used to provide the Room functionality and to generate Room statistics.

5. Guest Participants

Users may participate in certain Rooms without registering for a Music Fever account.
Guests choose a temporary username.
For guests, Music Fever stores only information necessary to represent their participation in the Room, such as:
  • Temporary username
  • Songs added to the Room
Music Fever does not require guests to provide an email address or create an account.

6. Playlists

Registered users may create private playlists.
Private playlists are visible only to their owner.
Administrators may also create public playlists that are available to Music Fever users.
Music Fever may store playlist names, included tracks, ownership information and dates associated with playlist creation or modification.

7. Statistics

Music Fever may generate personal statistics based on the user's activity.
Personal statistics are private and visible only to the corresponding user.
Closed Rooms may also contain statistics relating to that Room, including musical preferences, activity levels and participant contributions.

8. Why We Use This Information

Information is processed only where necessary to:
  • Create and maintain user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Provide Rooms and collaborative queues
  • Manage playlists
  • Process music requests
  • Provide playback functionality
  • Generate user and Room statistics
  • Maintain security and proper operation of the service
  • Support the academic development and evaluation of the project
Music Fever does not sell personal information.

9. External Services

Music Fever relies on external music services including:
  • Spotify
  • Apple Music
  • iTunes
Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.
Information sent directly to those services may therefore be processed independently by Spotify or Apple.

10. Cookies

Music Fever does not currently use advertising or behavioural tracking cookies.
Only cookies or equivalent technologies that are technically necessary for authentication, sessions, security or basic operation of the application may be used.
If analytics or additional tracking technologies are introduced later, this Privacy Policy will be updated.

11. Advertising and Payments

The academic version of Music Fever:
  • does not display advertising;
  • does not sell user information;
  • does not contain paid subscriptions or in-app purchases.
Future commercial versions may use different terms and privacy practices.

12. Data Retention

Personal information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Music Fever and fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.
Users may delete their account from their profile settings.
When an account is deleted, personal data associated with that account should be deleted or anonymised unless retention is required for technical, security or legal reasons.
Historical Room information may be anonymised where necessary to preserve aggregated Room statistics without identifying a deleted user.

13. Your Rights

Where the GDPR applies, users may have rights including:
  • access to their personal data;
  • correction of inaccurate data;
  • deletion of their data;
  • restriction of processing;
  • objection to certain processing;
  • data portability where applicable;
  • withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent.
Requests may be sent to: privacy@musicfever.com
The GDPR establishes these rights and requires controllers to facilitate their exercise.

14. Account Deletion

Users may delete their Music Fever account through the profile editing section.
Deleting an account will terminate access to registered-user functionality and initiate deletion or anonymisation of the associated personal information.

15. Security

Music Fever uses reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect account and activity information against unauthorised access, loss, modification or disclosure.
However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

16. Children

Music Fever is intended for users aged 14 or older.
Users under 14 should not create an account or provide personal data without the consent required by applicable law.
Under Spanish data-protection law, consent-based processing of a minor's personal data can generally rely on the minor's own consent from age 14.

17. Changes to this Privacy Policy

Music Fever's core functionality is expected to remain stable, although minor improvements and technical changes may be introduced.
This Privacy Policy may be updated when functionality, external integrations or legal requirements change.
The date at the top of the page will indicate the latest revision.
