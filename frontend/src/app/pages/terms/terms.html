5. Rooms

Rooms are collaborative music queues designed primarily for people sharing the same physical location.
A host creates a Room and participants join using the Room code or other supported invitation method.
Participants may contribute songs to the shared queue.
Music playback occurs through the host's authorised playback device or account.
Participants do not independently stream synchronized copies of the Room audio through Music Fever.
